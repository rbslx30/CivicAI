Citizen Rights & Awareness

Empowering you with the knowledge of your basic human rights.

🛡️

Raise Complaints Without Fear

Every citizen has the fundamental right to report grievances without facing retaliation or discrimination.

⚖️

Transparent & Fair Resolution

You have the right to a fair, unbiased process handled independently by the assigned department.

⏱️

Timely Response

You have the right to receive a prompt acknowledgment and estimated resolution timeline from authorities.

📱

Digital Accessibility

You have the right to digitally access public services conveniently from anywhere at any time.

🔔

Be Informed of Status

You maintain the right to be continuously informed about the ongoing status and updates of your complaint.
