Help & Support

How to submit a complaint

Click on the "Submit" tab in the navigation bar. Fill out your details, describe your issue, select the urgency, and press "Submit Grievance". Our AI will handle the rest.

How to track a complaint

Navigate to the "Track" tab. You can find the real-time status of your issue by entering either your generated Application Number or your registered Mobile Number.

What is an Application Number?

It is a unique identifier generated securely for your complaint (e.g. APP20261234...). You will receive this ID immediately after a successful submission.

What do statuses mean?

Pending: Captured & queued.
Under Review: Being actively analyzed by the department.
Resolved: Issue closed and addressed.
